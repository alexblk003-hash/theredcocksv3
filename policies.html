Website & Order Information

This page explains how this website and product enquiries work. For any order-specific question, please contact THE RED COCKS before confirming an order.

Terms of Use

Website content is for general product information and enquiries. Availability, product details, prices, delivery coverage and order acceptance are confirmed directly with THE RED COCKS. Adding an item to the cart creates an enquiry list only; it does not create a confirmed order.

Delivery & Shipping

Delivery availability, timing, charges and coverage are confirmed directly before an order is accepted. Please do not assume delivery to a particular address or at a particular time until you receive confirmation. Delivery details are discussed personally on WhatsApp or call, so that the correct product, quantity and address can be confirmed.

Payment & Order Confirmation

THE RED COCKS does not accept online payment through this website. This is an official product and order-information website for our business. Every order is confirmed directly with the owner before preparation or delivery.

Payment is handled hand-to-hand only, after the order details have been discussed and confirmed. Do not send money through an unknown link, QR code or third-party payment request claiming to represent THE RED COCKS.

Returns, Cancellations & Refunds

This website does not process online payments. Payment is arranged directly and hand-to-hand after the order details are confirmed. Any cancellation, return or refund request relating to a confirmed order must be discussed directly with THE RED COCKS and will be handled according to the details of that order and applicable requirements.

Privacy

The cart is saved in your own browser so that your selected enquiry items remain available on that device. When you choose to discuss an order, it opens WhatsApp. Please share only the information needed to discuss your enquiry.

The website also includes a chat feature. Avoid entering sensitive personal, financial or payment information into the chat.
