Website & Order Information

This page explains how this website and product enquiries work. For any order-specific question, please contact THE RED COCKS before confirming an order.

Terms of Use

Website content is for general product information and enquiries. Availability, product details, prices, delivery coverage and order acceptance are confirmed directly by email. Adding an item to the cart creates an enquiry list only; it does not create a confirmed order.

Delivery & Shipping

Delivery availability, timing, charges and coverage are confirmed directly before an order is accepted. Please do not assume delivery to a particular address or at a particular time until you receive confirmation.

Returns, Cancellations & Refunds

This website does not process online payments. Any cancellation, return or refund request relating to a confirmed order must be discussed directly with THE RED COCKS and will be handled according to the details of that order and applicable requirements.

Privacy

The cart is saved in your own browser so that your selected enquiry items remain available on that device. When you choose to send an enquiry, it opens your email application. Please share only the information needed to discuss your enquiry.

The website also includes a chat feature. Avoid entering sensitive personal, financial or payment information into the chat.
